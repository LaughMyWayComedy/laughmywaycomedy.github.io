Behind the scenes

About LaughMyWay Publisher

LaughMyWay Publisher is an owner-operated desktop tool used to publish finished LaughMyWay videos to the creator’s own YouTube channel. It is not a public sign-up service.

01

Connect the channel

Google OAuth confirms access to the selected YouTube channel and lets the tool read channel and video details needed to verify publishing.

02

Publish approved work

The tool uploads selected videos with their titles, descriptions, tags, and thumbnails. It can set or change a video’s visibility when the owner instructs it to.

03

Keep control local

Publishing records and OAuth credentials stay on the owner’s Windows computer. You can read the full privacy notice for details.
